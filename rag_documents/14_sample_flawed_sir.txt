What occurred prior to the incident?

Detailed description of incident:
Sam immediately asked to call his mother. Mr. Williams let him know his mother did not want to speak with him and would call him later this week. Sam proceeded to walk towards the phone. Mr. Williams blocked Sam's path. Mr. Jones, who was standing in the entrance, asked Resident #1 and Resident #2 to leave the office. Sam and Resident #1 had been in a fight yesterday. Resident #1's Christmas pass was not going to be approved so he was already upset. When Mr. Williams blocked his path, Sam started cussing (f*** you's, etc.), pushed past Mr. Williams, went to his room and slammed the door. Mr. Williams immediately followed him up the stairs. Sam continued cussing at Mr. Williams telling him to leave him the f*** alone. Mr. Williams told Sam he needed to come back downstairs to the living room and could not be in the room by himself. Both Mr. Williams and Mr. Jones could not fix lunch and watch him. Sam ran down the stairs and bumped into Resident #2, who immediately started yelling at Sam. Resident #1 also began yelling, telling Sam to stop being so disrespectful to Mr. Williams. Sam then tried to force his way past Mr. Jones, Resident #2, and Resident #1 to go back into the office. Resident #2 pushed Sam into the wall, and before the boys could begin to fight, Mr. Jones began a solo PRT, putting Sam face first into the wall. Mr. Williams, who was coming down the stairs, heard Sam say, "Stop scratching me." Mr. Williams moved past Mr. Jones and Sam, and told Resident #2 and Resident #1 to go into the living room. The restraint went on for approximately 25 min. during which time Sam screamed at Mr. Jones and Mr. Williams, threatening to kill them by name. When Sam finally calmed down, Mr. Jones let him go. Mr. Williams had called the therapist on call, Ms. Pratt, who had arrived just before Sam was released from the restraint. She asked Sam if he was calm enough to take a walk outside. Once they left the house, Mr. Williams could hear Sam screaming that he wanted to call his mother. When he opened the door, he saw Sam running across the lawn towards the school. Both Mr. Williams and Ms. Pratt ran after Sam and found him behind the school. Mr. Williams told Sam that if he did not return to the house, he would not be given phone privileges this week and would not be able to call his mother. Sam began cursing at Mr. Williams again and walking towards him in a threatening way. At that point, Mr. Williams initiated a restraint, requesting Ms. Pratt assist. They placed Sam in a two-person takedown. Sam began struggling and screaming at staff. Then he stopped talking and refused to talk with Mr. Williams or Ms. Pratt. After another 25 min. the nurse arrived and requested Sam be let out of the restraint. He calmly walked towards the house with the nurse. Once he was in the office, the nurse called his mother, who said she would speak to Sam. Sam and his mother spoke for 10 minutes before he hung up the phone and went into the living room and sat on the couch.

Action taken as a result of this incident:
Sam processed at length with multiple staff members.
Sam lost his phone privileges for the week.
Resident #1 and Resident #2 will serve Seven Days of No Off Campus Privileges.
