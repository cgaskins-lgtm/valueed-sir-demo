DESCRIPTION OF INCIDENT 
What occurred prior to the incident?Sam Johnson was in his room when he was called to the office to speak with staff. Resident #1 and Resident #2 were also there being administered medication by Mr. Williams. When Sam arrived, Mr. Williams informed him that his mother had called to say she would not be able to visit that weekend.Detailed description of incident.Sam immediately requested to call his mother. Mr. Williams informed Sam that his mother did not wish to speak with him at that time and would call later in the week.
Sam then walked toward the phone. Mr. Williams stepped in front of him to block access. Mr. Jones, who was standing in the doorway, instructed Resident #1 and Resident #2 to leave the office. Staff were aware that Sam and Resident #1 had been involved in a physical altercation the previous day. 
When Mr. Williams blocked his path, Sam began yelling profanities at staff, including repeated statements of ?f--- you.? Sam pushed past Mr. Williams, went upstairs to his room, and slammed the door. Mr. Williams immediately followed him upstairs. Sam continued yelling profanities and told Mr. Williams to ?leave me the f--- alone.? Mr. Williams instructed Sam to return downstairs to the living room and advised that he could not remain in his room alone because staff needed to maintain supervision.
Sam ran back downstairs and made contact with Resident #2, who immediately began yelling at him. Resident #1 also began yelling and told Sam to stop being disrespectful to Mr. Williams. Sam then attempted to force his way past Mr. Jones, Resident #2, and Resident #1 to re-enter the office. Before a fight could begin, Mr. Jones initiated a solo PRT and placed Sam against the wall in a face-forward hold. As Mr. Williams came downstairs, he heard Sam say, ?Stop scratching me.? Mr. Williams then directed Resident #1 and Resident #2 to go into the living room.
The restraint continued for approximately 25 minutes. During that time, Sam screamed at Mr. Jones and Mr. Williams and made threats to kill both staff members by name. Once Sam calmed, Mr. Jones released him from the restraint. Mr. Williams had contacted the on-call therapist, Ms. Pratt, who arrived shortly before Sam was released. Ms. Pratt asked Sam whether he was calm enough to take a walk outside, and Sam left the house with her.
After they exited the house, Mr. Williams heard Sam yelling that he wanted to call his mother. When Mr. Williams opened the door, he observed Sam running across the lawn toward the school. Mr. Williams and Ms. Pratt ran after him and located him behind the school. Mr. Williams instructed Sam to return to the house and told him that failure to do so would result in loss of phone privileges for the week and inability to call his mother.
Sam then began cursing at Mr. Williams again and walked toward him in a threatening manner. At that time, Mr. Williams initiated a restraint and requested assistance from Ms. Pratt. Staff placed Sam into a two-person takedown. Sam struggled and screamed at staff, and then became silent and refused to speak to either Mr. Williams or Ms. Pratt. Approximately 25 minutes later, the nurse arrived and requested that Sam be released from the restraint. Sam was released and walked calmly back to the house with the nurse.
Once in the office, the nurse contacted Sam?s mother, who agreed to speak with him. Sam spoke with his mother by phone for approximately 10 minutes. After the call ended, Sam went into the living room and sat on the couch.Action taken as a result of this incident.Sam processed at length with Nurse Finely. 
Sam lost his phone privileges for the week. 
John and Michael will serve Seven Days of No Off Campus Privileges.Name(s) of staff involved.Mr. Jonathon Williams, Mr. Romero Jones, Ms. Mikka Pratt, Nurse Lindsey FinelyWitnesses (other than staff involved).John Smith and Micheal White; Mr. Jonathon Williams, Mr. Romero Jones, Ms. Mikka Pratt, Nurse Lindsey FinelyWas there a physical restraint?
